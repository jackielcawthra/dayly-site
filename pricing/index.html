Pricing questions

Is the email forwarding included in every plan?

Yes, on both paid plans, with no per-item limit that you would ever reach in normal family life. The free tier is manual entry only.

What happens after the 30 days?

The account goes read-only — you can still see everything and export it. Nothing is deleted, and subscribing later picks up exactly where you left off.

Is there a discount for paying annually?

Yes, €58 a year rather than €72. There is no long contract either way and you can cancel at any point.

Do you offer a reduced price for anyone?

Yes — if the price is genuinely the reason you cannot use it, write to us and we will sort something out. We would rather a household used it than not.

What if we live in two houses?

The Two homes plan covers exactly that: one set of children, two households, presence handled by half-day so nothing falls due on a day a child is elsewhere.

Do children need to pay or have an account?

No. Children are included at no extra cost and open the app with a code rather than an email address.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you subscribe and it turns out not to suit your family, write within 30 days and we will refund it without a conversation about why.
