Editorial team

The Dayly Editors

The Dayly Guide is written and edited in-house by the people who build Dayly — parents, and a small editorial team. We are not clinicians, teachers or psychologists, and we do not claim to be. Where an article rests on evidence we link to the original source and say plainly what it does and does not show.

How we write

Every article in the Guide is written to the same standard, set out in our editorial policy. In short: we say what we know, we say what we are unsure about, and we link to the original source rather than to somebody else's summary of it. We do not invent credentials, and where a subject needs a clinician or a teacher, we say so and point you to one.
