How old are they?

Age is not the real test

The better question is whether the previous responsibility has become automatic — three weeks without you mentioning it. If it has, add. If it has not, wait, or make the current one smaller.

And start smaller than feels necessary. A child who owns eleven small things by ten has been practising for years and does not experience it as a burden. A child handed eleven things in September experiences a regime. More on this.
