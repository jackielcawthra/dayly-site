How Dayly works

Stop being the only one who knows what’s happening.

1

Forward it

Send Dayly anything — an email, newsletter, PDF, photo or pasted text. You can even set up a rule so school and club emails go straight to Dayly.

2

Dayly reads it

Dayly finds the dates, times, places, kit and deadlines, and works out who it belongs to. You approve it before it goes into the calendar.

3

Everyone sees what’s theirs

One family calendar, with a colour for each person. Everyone sees their own plans, responsibilities and what’s coming up — without having to ask you.

One view, a colour per person

School hours, clubs, appointments, matches, the days each child is at each house. All of it in one week, colour-coded, so a clash is visible before it happens rather than on the day.

And the things a calendar cannot hold

Most of what makes a family week difficult has no start time:

  • Thursday is a kit day
  • The reading folder goes back on Wednesday
  • The bins go out on Friday
  • Emma’s washing day is Tuesday
  • The hallway is Noah’s until 1 May
  • The trip form has to be signed by the 14th

In an ordinary calendar these become either all-day events that clutter the view or nothing at all. In Dayly they are recurring responsibilities with owners, which is what they actually are.

What children see

A child’s calendar shows their own things, in their own words. Not “E. training 17:30 Ekhagen” — “Football, 17:30, Dad is driving”.

From about eleven, most children can maintain their own entries, which is the point at which a family calendar stops being a notice board and starts being a tool they use.

Working with the calendar you already have

Dayly exports to a standard calendar file, so family events can appear alongside a work diary without anyone having to duplicate them. We would rather fit around what you use than insist you move everything.

Common questions

Does it sync with Google or Apple Calendar?

Dayly exports events as a standard .ics file that any calendar can subscribe to. Two-way live sync is not currently built — we would rather ship it properly than half-ship it.

Can each child see only their own things?

Yes. A child’s view shows their own events plus the household things that concern everyone.
