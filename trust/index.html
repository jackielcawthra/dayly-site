Trust & safety

Because there are children in it

Dayly holds information about children. That places obligations on us that an ordinary productivity app does not have, and this page is where we set out what we do about them.

What Dayly stores

The information a household enters: names, the days people are here, jobs and who owns them, calendar entries, school terms and kit days, shopping and meals, trips and packing lists, and the record of what was ticked and when.

That is all household-generated. We do not buy data, enrich it, or combine it with anything from elsewhere.

What Dayly does not do

  • No advertising. Not in a parent’s view, not in a child’s, not anywhere.
  • No profiling. We do not build behavioural profiles of children or adults.
  • No third-party trackers in the app. No advertising SDKs, no session recording, no data brokers.
  • No selling or sharing data with anyone, for any purpose, ever.
  • No location tracking. Dayly does not know where anyone is.
  • No health, biometric or special-category data. We do not ask for it and there is nowhere to put it.

Children’s information

A child in Dayly does not need an email address or a phone number. They open the app with a code that an adult in the household sets. There is no public profile, no messaging with anyone outside the household, and nothing a child enters leaves it.

The full detail is on the children’s data page, including what a parent can and cannot see.

Parental control

The adults in a household control everything: who is in it, what each person can see, which notes are adults-only, and what happens to the data. A child cannot switch to another person’s view.

We deliberately do not provide a surveillance view — no monitoring dashboard, no reporting on a child’s failures. A parent can see what is outstanding, because that is the point of a shared household. Anything beyond that would change what the product is.

Security

  • Hosted in the EU
  • Encrypted in transit, and at rest
  • Access to production data limited to the people who need it, and logged
  • Regular backups, with a tested restore
  • No production data on anyone’s laptop

We are a small team and we would rather say what we actually do than imply a certification we do not hold. If you need specifics for your own assessment, write to us and we will answer properly.

GDPR

Dayly is built under EU data protection law rather than adapted to it. In practice that means:

  • You can see everything we hold, because it is what you entered
  • You can export the whole household as a file, at any time
  • You can delete it, and deletion means deletion — not deactivation
  • We keep it for as long as you have an account, and for a short, stated period afterwards for
  • backups, then no longer

To exercise any of this, write to hello@dayly.app and we will do it.

Transparent pricing

One price per family, no per-child fee, no upsell to see your own week, and no free tier that quietly stops working. If you stop paying, the account goes read-only and you can still export everything.

If something goes wrong

If there is ever a breach affecting your household, we will tell you what happened, what was affected and what we are doing about it — directly, and not in a paragraph at the bottom of a newsletter.

Common questions

Can my child see other children’s information?

A child sees their own view, and the shared household things — the week, the meals, the shopping list. They cannot switch to a sibling’s profile, and notes marked adults-only do not appear at all.

Do you use AI on our data?

We do not train models on household data, and we do not send it to third-party model providers. If that ever changes it will be opt-in, explained plainly, and off by default.

What if I want everything deleted?

Write to us and we will delete the household, permanently, and confirm when it is done. Backups roll off within thirty days.
