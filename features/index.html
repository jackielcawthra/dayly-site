How Dayly works

Stop being the only one who knows what’s happening.

1

Forward it

Send Dayly anything — an email, newsletter, PDF, photo or pasted text. You can even set up a rule so school and club emails go straight to Dayly.

2

Dayly reads it

Dayly finds the dates, times, places, kit and deadlines, and works out who it belongs to. You approve it before it goes into the calendar.

3

Everyone sees what’s theirs

One family calendar, with a colour for each person. Everyone sees their own plans, responsibilities and what’s coming up — without having to ask you.

See how it works, in detail →

Forward it

Turn a letter into a plan

Send an email, a PDF, a photo of a letter or pasted text. Get back the dates, the kit, the money and the deadlines, on the right child. Tap to approve, and it is in the week.

See the week

Know what is coming without asking anyone

One week, a colour per person. Open it in the morning and see who needs to be where, what is due and what is different about today.

Hand things over

Give a child a job that is actually theirs

Make a job belong to a person rather than a rota, so nobody has to check whose turn it is. Give someone a room to keep, with an agreed standard.

Track what got done

See what is outstanding, without standing over anyone

Children tick off their own. You see what is still open, when a room was last reset, and who has been carrying the week.

Earn points together

Build one total the whole household shares

Everyone contributes to the same running total, and anyone can thank somebody for something that helped. No streaks, no ranking one child against another.

Say who is home

Stop things falling due when someone is away

Mark presence half-day by half-day — home for dinner, away at breakfast. Alternating weeks, one night a fortnight, a different pattern in the holidays.

Stay on top of school

Catch the kit day before the morning it matters

What is needed tomorrow, what is due this week, when term starts and ends. The PE bag, the form and the forty kronor all get a place and a person.

Feed everybody

Decide who cooks, and let anyone add to the list

Set a cook rota that already knows how many people are home. The shopping list is one the children can write to, so noticing the milk is not one person’s job.

Pack a trip

Let everyone pack their own bag

Give each traveller their own list and let them tick it off. You keep the passports, the insurance and the things that genuinely need an adult.

By design

What Dayly does not do to your family.

No location tracking

Dayly never knows where anybody is. It is not a gap we intend to close.

Nobody outside the household

No usernames, no friend requests, no way for a stranger to reach a child.

No monitoring view

A parent can see what is outstanding. There is no dashboard of a child’s failures.

Calendars export as a standard .ics feed. Everything else lives in Dayly, and leaves with you whenever you ask — see how we handle your family’s data.

The fastest way in is the setup quiz.

The same questions we would ask if we were building your household by hand — about twenty minutes, and it produces a written brief you can work from.
