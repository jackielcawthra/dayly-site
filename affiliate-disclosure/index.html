Disclosure

Affiliate policy

Some links earn us a commission. Here is exactly how that works and what it does not affect.

What an affiliate link is

If you follow certain links from the Dayly Guide and subscribe to or buy something, we may receive a small commission from that company. It never costs you more, and it is often the same price you would pay going directly.

Where they appear

Only in articles that recommend or compare products, and always with a disclosure at the top of the article — not buried at the bottom or written in grey.

Our rules

We only include things we would recommend anyway. If we would not tell a friend to use it, it does not go in, whatever the commission.

We say where something is weak. Every recommendation includes what it is bad at. A review with no criticism in it is an advertisement.

Commission does not affect order or inclusion. We do not rank by payout, and products with no affiliate programme appear alongside ones that pay.

We name our own product as ours. Dayly appears in categories where it competes. When it does, we say plainly that we make it, and we say who it is the wrong choice for.

We do not accept payment for coverage. No sponsored articles, no paid placements, no “partner content” dressed as editorial. If that ever changes, it will be labelled as advertising in a way nobody could miss.

We do not recommend anything for children that we would not let our own use. Apps with heavy monetisation aimed at children, advertising in a child’s view, or manipulative retention mechanics do not appear regardless of what they pay.

Why we do it at all

It funds the writing. The Guide takes real time to research and write, it is free to read, and there is no advertising on it. Affiliate income and Dayly subscriptions are what pay for it.

We think that is a reasonable arrangement provided it is disclosed clearly and the recommendations are honest. If you ever think a piece has been shaped by a commission, tell us at hello@dayly.app — it is the criticism we would most want to hear.
