What it is

Dayly takes that work off your plate. It turns the information you already receive into dates, deadlines, reminders, responsibilities and things to do — in the right place, for the right person.

Frequently
asked
questions

What can I forward?

Emails, newsletters, PDFs, photographs of paper letters, and text you paste in. If a person could read it and work out what it means, that is the target.

Does it get it right?

Not always — reading a badly written newsletter is hard for people too. So nothing reaches your diary until you tick it, every row shows the sentence it came from, and anything it was unsure of is separated out with the reason. Approving a whole newsletter takes about thirty seconds.

Do I have to forward things manually?

You can, or you can set up a rule in your email so the school and the clubs go straight through. Most people start manual and automate the two or three senders that matter.

What about things nobody emails?

Add them the normal way — the app is a full family organiser underneath. Forwarding is the fastest route in, not the only one.

Do the children need a phone?

No. It works on any device with a browser, including a shared tablet in the kitchen. Children open it with their own code rather than an account.

What does it cost?

One price for the whole household, however many of you there are — everything is included, and adding a child never costs more. Free for thirty days, and we do not ask for a card to start. Pricing in full.
