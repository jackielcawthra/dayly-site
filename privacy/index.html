Privacy

Privacy policy

Written to be read. If anything here is unclear, write to us and we will fix the wording.

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Who we are

Dayly, a company registered in Sweden. Contact: hello@dayly.app. We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

Information you give us. The account email of the adult who sets up the household, and everything the household enters into Dayly: names, presence, jobs, calendar entries, school information, shopping, meals, trips, and the record of what was completed.

Information from using the site and app. Basic technical data — device type, browser, approximate region from your IP address, and which pages were opened. We use privacy-respecting analytics and we do not use advertising cookies.

What we do not collect. Location, contacts, photos, health data, biometric data, or anything from other services.

Why we use it

PurposeBasis
Running the service you signed up forPerformance of a contract
Keeping it secure and preventing abuseLegitimate interests
Answering your emailsLegitimate interests
Sending the newsletter, if you asked for itConsent
Meeting legal and accounting obligationsLegal obligation

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run Dayly — hosting, email delivery, payment processing — each under a data processing agreement, and each limited to what they need.

We do not sell data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not share it with schools, employers or anyone else who asks, unless legally compelled, in which case we will tell you unless we are prohibited from doing so.

Where it lives

In the EU. Where a provider processes data outside the EU, that transfer is covered by the standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account, plus up to thirty days in backups after deletion. Accounting records are kept for the period Swedish law requires.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent. Write to hello@dayly.app and we will act within thirty days, usually much sooner. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Cookies

We use what is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences, plus privacy-respecting analytics that do not identify you personally. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no consent banner asking you to accept things you do not want.

Children

There is a separate page on how children’s information is handled, because it deserves more than a paragraph.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will email you rather than quietly updating the date at the top.
