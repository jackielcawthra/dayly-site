What the score means

It is not a diagnosis. It is a way of turning an argument about fairness into a list you can both look at, which is most of the work. The number matters far less than the two or three areas it concentrates in — those are the ones worth moving.

The mental load is the cognitive work of running a household: noticing, remembering, planning and deciding, as distinct from the physical task. It is invisible, continuous, and in most households it concentrates in one person. The longer explanation.

Common questions

Should both parents do this?

Yes, separately, and then compare. The disagreement is usually not about the totals — it is that each person has lines the other did not think to consider.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and closing the tab loses it.
